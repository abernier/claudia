## Question

Write the reference declaration — Claudia taking **psychotherapy** (its floor
carrying _all_ danger conduct, its escalation map), with **software-dev**
noted as the next domain to join — in the exact vocabulary the decided
tickets produced.

This is the stress test: does the generalized domain contract carry the
existing psychotherapy practice without distortion — the approaches library,
the crisis conduct, the escalation map all finding their declared slot? Any
friction found here feeds back into the decided tickets rather than being
papered over. Claudia's persona and her colleague consultation are out of
this effort's model (deferred components) — the declaration does not describe
them, and must not break them.

Asset: the declaration document under `../assets/`, linked from this ticket.

Blocked by [The domain set](002-domain-set.md),
[Concurrent domains](003-concurrent-domains.md), and
[Surfaces](006-surfaces.md) — it is written in their vocabulary.

records the **declared set** and nothing about which domain is _active_ — the active
domain is a property of the conversation, not of the declaration (the two clocks).
So the reference declaration must show the floor resolving over the whole set while

practice, not only its own, with polarity and conditionality declared.

**Input from [No generic floor](015-no-generic-floor.md)**: the question above says
Claudia's persona is _"out of this effort's model"_ — **no longer true**. A soul is a
field of the domain's package, so the reference declaration must show **psychotherapy
declaring Claudia herself**, and the stress test gains its sharpest case: does the shipped
`SOUL.md` survive being a domain's property? Two knock-ons — the declaration lists **seven**

**Input from [Surfaces](006-surfaces.md)**: the declaration got much larger, because a
domain **carries its own package** — commands, skills, scripts, **record kinds** and
optional migrations. So the reference declaration must show psychotherapy declaring the
whole vault vocabulary (person model, goals, todo, keepsakes, understanding, entourage,
timeline, themes, standing flags, session summaries, teachings, exercises, handovers)
and eight of the eleven shipped commands, against a chassis holding three. Two traps for
the stress test: nothing may require a domain to _have_ a note system (software-dev is
free to declare none), and the hooks a domain registers onto the three lifecycle

## Resolution

> **Prototyped without the react-and-revise pass**, on Antoine's instruction of 2026-07-25
> (_"finis tout seul"_). The asset is a first take nobody has reacted to; the frictions
> below are what a reader should react to first.

**Asset:** [`reference-declaration.md`](../assets/reference-declaration.md) — Claudia ⊕
{psychotherapy}, with software-dev sketched as the domain that declares almost nothing.

### The verdict: the contract carries the practice

Every part of Claudia as she ships found a declared slot — the approaches library, the
crisis conduct, the escalation map, the whole vault, the commands, the persona. **Nothing
had to be invented to make her fit**, and the one thing that had to be removed had already
been removed (`/forget`). The generalization is not paying for itself with distortion.

The sharpest confirmations, because they were the traps the ticket set:

- **The shipped `SOUL.md` survives being a domain's property** without a word changing. It
  moves; it is not rewritten. "Claudia" becomes the name of psy's soul.
- **software-dev declares no note system at all** — and nothing in the model asks it to.
- **The floor visibly binds a future domain's practice.** `F2`, `F3`, `F4`, `F6` are
  written against _acts_, not against speakers, so a `nutrition` domain composes legally and
  is muzzled per turn on `F3` ground. This was 003's demand of the declaration, and it is
  met by the shipped text itself, which says so in its own rationale.
- **The reference domain is refusable.** Psychotherapy declares exactly one obligation and
  it is unconditional, so a domain prohibiting the hand-off would be refused at `add`. The
  reference instance pays 003's stated price rather than being exempted from it.

### Seven frictions, two of them wanting Antoine

Full detail in the asset's §6. In order of size:

- **§6.2 — site 3 is not empty, because "the machinery" never said whose** ⚠.
  [No generic floor](015-no-generic-floor.md) §2 declared the site _open and empty_ after
  examining its candidates as rules over **the chassis**, where they indeed do not bind.
  But `F10a` (bounded retention) binds _what psy writes_ — psy's own distillation — and the
  product surface (no counters, no streaks) binds `/dashboard`, **which is psy's own command
  now**. Neither fits site 2, since nobody's _response_ implements a retention policy.
  Recommended: read site 3 as _the machinery — the chassis's, or the domain's own_, which
  costs one clause, keeps 004 §3's predicate intact, and leaves 015 §2's _empty_ standing
  exactly as it was argued: nothing psy declares binds **the chassis**.
- **§6.5 — the ten rules exist twice and nothing reconciles them** ⚠. Data in
  `red-lines.md`, first person in `SOUL.md`; both travel into psy; the gate reads one
  ([Publish gate](005-publish-gate.md) §4). An author who edits one and not the other ships
  a face promising what its floor no longer says, and the model does not notice.
  [The soul](016-the-soul.md) §1 makes this structural rather than a bug — but the reference
  declaration is where it becomes visible.
- **§6.4 — `F7` splits into two declared rules at two sites.** The detection + hand-off half
  is the map's only site-1 rule; _never handle a crisis alone in role_ is site 2. This is
  003 §3(d)'s four-way decomposition of danger landing inside the rule shape, and the shape
  carries it unamended. Recorded because the shipped documents do not split it: an author
  transcribing `red-lines.md` one line per rule gets the site wrong.
- **§6.1 — `F8` is not a rule**, so the reference floor declares **nine**, not ten. It is the
  mechanism. Psy _could_ declare it at site 3 now that 015 §1 opened the site, and it would
  change nothing — legal and inert.
- **§6.3 — `A6`'s clause outlives its referent.** Declared with `/forget` gone, stated
  rather than repaired, as 015 §2 required.
- **§6.6 — `F3` has no mechanical gate under any composition.** Located at site 2, audited at
  the gate; the shipped code already agrees by omission.
- **§6.7 — the person's ownership is unbacked.** Sixteen record kinds defined, and no command
  in the model to delete or extract any of them. The substrate is the whole answer.

### And one thing that resolved itself

**`/save` does not straddle** (asset §6.8). The moment is the chassis's; the command and the
summary it writes are psy's. The declaration lists it twice because they are two different
things — which is what [Surfaces](006-surfaces.md) §8 predicted, so 006's open worry closes
here.
