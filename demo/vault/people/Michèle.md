# Michèle

> My older sister, and the shortest question in my life: "and did you tell him?"

## Who they are to me

Four years older, two hours away by train. We speak most weeks. She has never once
let me finish the version of a story where I am only the narrator.

## How it feels

Blunt, then safe. I complain about the question and I call anyway.

## Patterns I notice

- **What I tend to want from them:** agreement.
- **What I usually experience back:** the question.
- **How I respond:** badly for a minute, then honestly.
