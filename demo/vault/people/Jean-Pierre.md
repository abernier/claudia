# Jean-Pierre

> Twelve years, and he still has no idea he has been graded.

## Who they are to me

My husband since 2014. Literal in the best and the worst way: he answers the
question he was asked, and only that one. Cooks without being asked, spots a broken
hinge across a room, and does not spot a cold wife at all.

## How it feels

Warm, easily. Then, once a week or so, like standing in front of someone holding a
sign that faces away from them.

## Patterns I notice

(tentative, mine to correct:)

- **What I tend to want from them:** to be read, so that asking is not necessary.
- **What I usually experience back:** exactly what I said, taken at face value.
- **How I respond:** I keep score quietly, then present the total over something tiny.
