# Where we are — working understanding

_Provisional, {{TODAY-3}} — yours to correct, always._

She can already describe the shape better than I can: she wants to be noticed before
she has to ask, keeps a private tally while she waits, then hands over the total in
the form of a small grievance — a filter, a thermostat, a menu. The argument is never
about the object, and everyone in the room can tell.

What loosens it is not insight but timing. Said in the first five minutes, the plain
version costs about four seconds and works — she has evidence with Sylvie, with
Michèle, with everyone she is not married to. Said after the tally has started, the
same sentence arrives as a verdict, and the evening is gone.

What we are watching: whether it can be said at home while the thing is still small.
That is the next place this gets real.
