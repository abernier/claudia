# {{TODAY-16}} — the dryer, and what it was not about

First conversation. Agnès arrived the morning after a forty-minute argument about
the tumble dryer, which had not been about the tumble dryer. What she wanted was for
him to notice she had carried the whole week; what she said was that nobody ever
empties the filter.

She named the pattern herself, laughing at it: **my telepathy problem**. She sets an
exam, she marks the exam, and he never gets told he is sitting one. Her sister
Michèle had already asked the question that ends it — "and did you actually tell
him?" — which Agnès reports as unbearable and correct.

What mattered: hearing that the wanting is not the problem. She is allowed to want
to be noticed. What costs her the evening is the silent scoring around it.

Agreed to work toward: catching it while it is still small, and saying the plain
version once.

Follow-ups: notice where else this happens — she suspects it is only at home.
