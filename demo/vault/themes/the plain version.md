## Exceptions / what helps

- It only works early. After the tally has started, the same sentence lands as a verdict.
- It is much easier with everyone who is not him — which is data, not a failing.

## Tentative reflection (mine to correct)

Every time she has used it, the answer arrived faster than the argument would have.
Saying it costs about four seconds. Not saying it is, so far, measured in evenings.
