**Claudia no longer steps into sessions that have nothing to do with her.** Her
skills are installed for every Claude Code session on your machine, and some of them
could be picked up there: saying "I don't know what to do" in the middle of coding,
or simply writing her name while working on something about her, could bring her in.

Now she comes only when you speak to her: start a message with her name ("Claudia,
…", "Hey Claudia", or just "Claudia"), write "@Claudia", or ask to talk to her.
Everything else she does, including the crisis support, happens only once she is
there, and the slash commands run only when you type them.

If you used to reach her just by opening up, without her name, say her name first.
